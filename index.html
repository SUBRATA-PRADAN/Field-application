How to set it up (step-by-step)+
  1. Open Survey123Tap the button above — it opens the app via the organization portal. Not installed? You'll be sent to the Play Store.
  2. Log inSign in with the ID and password shared for your geography/team.
  3. Tap your profileTap the profile icon in the top corner of the app.
  4. Choose "Download Survey"You'll see the list of all available surveys.
  5. Download what you needDownload the survey you want to fill right now — this is a one-time step.
  6. Start filling, update now and thenYou won't need to download it again. If a survey changes later, just sync/update it.
